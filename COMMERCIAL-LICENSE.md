[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). That license allows any
**noncommercial** use — personal study, hobby projects, research, teaching, and
use by charitable, educational, public-research, public-safety/health,
environmental or government organizations.

## What needs a commercial license

A separate written license is required for any commercial purpose, for example:

- selling Keystone or a modified version of it, or charging for copies;
- sublicensing it, or bundling it (code or content) into a product you sell;
- offering it as a paid, hosted or managed service (SaaS), or running it as part
  of a paid training, coaching or consulting offering;
- using it inside a for-profit company for its business operations
  (e.g. internal employee training programs).

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask — see below.

## How to request one

1. Open a new issue in this repository:
   <https://github.com/WestMoneyDE/keystone-architect/issues/new>
2. Title it **`Commercial license request`**.
3. Briefly describe:
   - who you are (company or project name),
   - the intended use (internal use, hosted service, redistribution, …),
   - the rough scale (number of users/installations) and timeframe.

Please **do not** put confidential information in the public issue. If details
are needed, the maintainer will reply in the issue and agree on a private channel
with you there.

Until you have a signed commercial license, only the noncommercial terms of the
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE) apply.
